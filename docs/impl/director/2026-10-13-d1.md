# D1: safe nudges, 2026-10-13

D1 adds four bias hooks (N4, N6, N8, N9), the fair-dice gate, a daily planner, and the Almanac as a second selectable personality. **The Land Itself (budget 0) is still the default** everywhere: the CLI, play, the web, tests and goldens.

## What is built

**Biases are data.** They live in `World.director.plan.biases`. Each bias has an expiry, a source thread and move, a cost and an amount. Each owning module consults them through its own `api.ts` hook at the moment it decides. With no live bias, every hook returns "no bias". The generic forms are in `director/bias.ts`.

| Hook | Nudge | Owner and decision | Envelope |
|---|---|---|---|
| `belief.carrierWeight` | N4 | The carrier's own topic selection in `planTransmit` | +≤50% on a topic already worth telling. Never applies to a kind (people) predicate or a charge, to a forced/deliberate telling, or to a fact the carrier does not hold |
| `social.optionTilt` | N8 | Where `companyUrge` seeks company; whom `activity.callTarget` calls on | The favoured option scores within 15% of the best (ties go to it). A call may move only onto a favoured household |
| `social.windowPick` | N6 | When an item loan's breach lands (`loansDaily`) | Inside its own window, [due + 12 h, due + 3 d]. The default is due + 1 d, as before |
| `fae.thresholdTilt` | N9 | Noticing an omen; finding a draw shortfall | DC −2 at most |

**Fair dice.** `pcPerceives` returns nothing when the PC takes part in the decision, or when any party (or the place itself) shares the PC's place at that moment. This is the same place test `social.perceivedEvents` applies. It is enforced inside `liveBiases`, so no hook can bypass it.

**Legibility.** A director term (`source: 'director'`) appears only on off-screen Explains (the N4 telling, the N9 checks). N8 and N6 leave the owner's Explain untouched, because the eventual arrival may be seen.

**The Ledger.** When a consult changes an outcome, the owner calls `noteNudge`. That writes `{t, nudge, bias, process, target, before, after, rationale{threadId, τ, move, pacing}, cost, foresaw, knowledge:'omniscient'}`:
- Hourly re-decisions are de-duplicated per day.
- At most 128 rows are retained; older rows are counted, not kept.
- "Show the hand" means `directorReport().nudges`: budget, totals, cycles, live biases and the Ledger. It appears in god observations and CLI manifests, never in fair views.

**Planner** (`director/plan.ts`, daily):
- Expire old biases, accrue budget (cap 30), and run the death-spiral check. `τ_slow > 800` for 3 days records `mercy_wanted`; N10 itself is D2.
- Rank the top-k open threads by interest and gather their moves from `content/base/director/moves.toml`. There are 16 moves, as data per Chronicle pattern.
- Filter the moves by pacing:
  - RELAX: social, opportunity and discovery moves, plus N6 delays that hold threats back.
  - BUILD: the top threat thread, chosen from threads and τ before any roll is examined, with its N4 foreshadowing.
  - PEAK: only N6 delays on other threads.
  - FADE: aftermath (social and opportunity).
- Greedy knapsack by value/cost, where value = interest × the category multiplier. Ties break by thread id, then move id.
- A move places one bias per participant (or up to three facts for N4). Its cost is charged once per group.

**Foresight:**
- `off` pays at placement.
- `tiltable` (the default) reserves the cost and charges only when a tilt actually changes an outcome. N9 records `foresaw: true`.
- `full` is recorded but behaves like tiltable in D1.

**The Almanac's seasonal curve:**
- RELAX lasts 5/6/5/3 days (spring/summer/autumn/winter), then BUILD.
- BUILD goes to PEAK when the target thread gains a gun the PC has seen, or when `τ_fast ≥ 700`. After 3 days without either, it fizzles to FADE.
- PEAK lasts 12 h, then FADE, then RELAX.
- The Land Itself keeps D0's classifier byte for byte.

**Settings:**
- CLI: `--director almanac` on `run` and `play` (`--new` sessions included). `--director-personality` remains as an alias.
- Web: `?director=almanac` for a new game.
- The choice is recorded in `World.director.settings`, and therefore in the manifest and the save.
- Any other personality throws ("arrives in D2").

## Hard prohibitions (05 §8.3) and tests

`packages/cli/test/director-d1.test.ts`:
- **Fair dice.** Iron Ford 42 runs 30 days with the Almanac, budget forced to the cap every hour. Every event `perceivedEvents` gives the PC carries no director term anywhere in its Explain tree, children and DC terms included. Nudges did take effect; every Ledger row is omniscient and none targets the PC.
- **Gate unit.** All four hooks return nothing when the PC is a party or present, and something when it is not. The 15% and DC −2 caps hold, and `windowPick` clamps to its window.
- **People and culture.** No file in `director/` reads `ancestryId`, `cultureId`, peoples or cultures. Faekin: the move schema refuses `faekin_suspicion`, `scapegoat` and `defense_fired`, and the planner refuses a forged high-interest `faekin_suspicion` thread. N4 refuses `faekin` (`kind_of`) and `witch` (charge) even when a bias names them.
- **Psyche and other slices.** Director imports only `anxietyOf` from psyche. `planDay` plus `directorSystem` leave the stripped world hash unchanged; only `world.director` is written.
- **No fabricated rumors.** Every N4 bias names a fact held, unconcealed, by a living non-PC carrier. The hook only re-weights the carrier's own beliefs.
- **Land Itself.** No `plan` slice is created. Stripped-world parity holds against the pre-D1 tree (Iron Ford 42 and town 7, 30 d: `06f80d981d2248db` / `2780d586ed8bde25`, Chronicles identical). The Almanac's save/restore continues identically. Foresight `off` and `tiltable` account as described.
- **Existing tests.** The D0 test now expects `hearthside`, not `almanac`, to throw.

- **Monthly `stats.csv`.** Hunger, rest, warmth, cash and kcal track land_itself within normal run-to-run drift. Neither town has a starvation or death spike: 40/40 and 24/24 alive all year, and survival deaths are 0 in both runs.
- **N6 and mercy.** N6 was never placed: no open item loan involved a top thread's participant. No mercy was wanted.
- **Cost.** Director cost stays under 0.5 ms/h. Hook consults are charged to their owning systems; total ms/h (almanac vs land_itself) was 30.15 vs 30.44 (Iron Ford) and 22.86 vs 21.34 (town 7) on a loaded machine.

**Reading it honestly.** D1's levers are deliberately small. For passive PCs they do not move dead air:
- Iron Ford is +11 h, town 7 is −12 h and 9 fewer quiet days, which is within noise.
- Cycles mostly fizzle: 44 of 45 and 43 of 45 BUILDs never reached the PC. The seasonal machine is visible, but the PEAK it waits for rarely comes.
- Under tiltable foresight the budget sits at its 30 cap, because placed tilts rarely flip anything. N4 flips about 10% of placements and N8 about 3–8%.

The D0 caveat stands: commanded play, not these fixtures, is where foreshadowing can land.
