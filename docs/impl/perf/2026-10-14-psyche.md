## Proposal 5: incremental memory digests (exact)

`social/memory.ts` is now the only code that writes memories, and it keeps each owner's digests (`memoryDigests`: per remembered person, the max salience per kind done and did, and the negative total) equal to the full scan:
- **New row:** folded in (max and total are monotone).
- **Removed row** (evicted at the cap, forgotten, consolidated): folded out when it sits below its kind's max and the worst negative. If it is at a max, only that (owner, about) pair is rescanned. If the pair has no rows left, it is dropped.
- **Any salience change** (daily decay, softening, Sunday consolidation, backstory ageing): drops the owner's cache.

The cache is derived and never saved. It is keyed by the `social.memories` table object, so loaded, forked and cloned worlds start cold, while owner-local facades that share the table (dormant upkeep) share its cache. Content is checked by identity. Digests are copy-on-write.

How it is enforced:
- **Types:** `Memory` fields, `social.memories` and `social.memoriesByOwner` are `readonly`. A write anywhere else fails typecheck. Initial empty lists go through `ensureMemoryOwner`, backstory decay through `ageSeededMemory`, and fixtures through `replaceOwnerMemories`. `MemoryInput` stays writable.
- **Grep guard:** `packages/cli/test/memory-digests.test.ts` scans core, play, cli (src and tests) and web. It rejects casts of the memory tables, assignments into them or deletes from them, owner-list mutation, table replacement, and `Writable<Memory>` casts anywhere outside memory.ts. A second case proves that the guard matches each forbidden shape.
- **Equivalence:** the same test compares cached digests to the full scan for every owner, every hour of a 9-day run. That run covers nightly decay, eviction and Sunday consolidation, and is followed by a JSON save/restore and 48 more hours. Disabling the insert fold-in makes it fail at hour 7.

An invariant-script rule was not used: `scripts/check-invariants.ts` is foundation-owned, and core forbids `WeakMap` (this cache follows the bounded `Map` pattern of `social/pairs.ts`).

## Proposal 2: psyche cadence for idle minds (negative result; prototype in `b2ed909`, removed)

In the prototype (`psyche/cadence.ts` plus `system.ts idleHour`), on an idle hour the mind does not gather proposals, scan rules or prohibitions, or arbitrate. It renews the thoughts the last pass renewed, from their own record: a gathered thought (`ext:`/`rule:`/`forbid:`) whose only fading is one hour's step gets its reasons back without that step, which is exactly its renewed state. It then books the same frustration, persona strain and discharge, fires a defense if one comes due (building the full Mind only then), and ages every thought. `chosen` keeps its own `at` and Explain, because it is the arbitration that actually ran. Thought reasons still sum to intensities.

An hour is idle only when all of the following hold. Otherwise the full pass runs that hour.
1. Processing is hourly, never a daily batch. The actor is asleep, and `chosen` is that same activity, voiced plain or standing (never a command, compromise, sublimation or impulse).
2. The last pass ran exactly one hour ago, fired no defense, and no defense is due.
3. Nothing happened to the actor since the last pass. The check reads events since a saved id watermark, plus this hour's commands:
   - any Command naming them, as the commanded actor or its target;
   - any event naming them in a non-agent role (in a private event, only target, patient, victim, listener or beneficiary; secrets are unperceived);
   - any public or witnessed event at their place (spoken to, struck, a hearth or fire, an omen, a scene), except routine `activity.started` and `activity.moved` (presence is in the signature).
   - Inner events never count.
4. The situation signature (32-bit mix of integers in fixed order) is unchanged. It covers:
   - place, activity, whether the lock still runs (**a lock ending is a full pass**), and whether the actor is held;
   - the people present;
   - the calendar routine's activity and duty (**a scheduled block change**);
   - every need's band, over all content thresholds (need bands and urges, drive `at`s, `need_below`s, 500);
   - the anxiety band, the night (it ends at 05:00), Sabbath, and the weekday if any `when` names weekdays.
5. No applicable content hour window opens or closes.
6. The choice is not close. At the last full pass no compromise or sublimation check was rolled, and the choice's base score led every rival by twice the noise amplitude, plus the compromise window, plus 60 per hour since that pass (needs drift inside their bands). No drive streak is old enough to roll for sublimation.

Psyche API writes from other modules drop the pace record, so the next hour is a full pass: `addThought`, `addAnxiety`, a displaced act landing, a discarded duty, and agency or standing-order changes. The bookkeeping is integers in World (`ActorPsyche.pace = {at, sig, slack, full}`, `PsycheState.seenEventId`), so restore continues exactly. The PC follows the same rule (P2), and any order forces the full pass that hour.

**Why it does not pay.** Diagnosed over 10 Iron Ford days (3,300 asleep actor-hours):
- 551 asleep hours are the first hour asleep.
- 1,730 fail the signature: 491 lock endings (sleep holds 4 h), 324 at the 05:00 night end, 301 routine changes, about 400 need-band crossings (Rest rising at 22–23 h, Thirst falling at 1–4 h), 101 Sabbath flips, 56 company changes and 41 anxiety bands.
- 279 fail the slack test and 66 the sublimation streak.
- 580 are idle.

Without condition 6 the idle count was 941. But shadow runs (fork each hour, run it with the cadence off, compare choices) showed 7% of those idle hours choosing differently, mostly sleepers who would have got up at 1–4 a.m. to drink. With condition 6 that error is 0.34% (seed 42) and 0.31% (seed 7). The signature and watermark cost roughly what the idle hours save. Making it pay would mean dropping wake-up conditions the brief requires, and Iron Ford's profile has no larger idle population: locked-and-alone awake hours were 5 in 10 days.

Events per kind: the summed absolute per-kind shift against base is smaller for the cadence than for the noise control in all three towns (Iron Ford 8,771 vs 12,421; gen 7 6,846 vs 12,238; gen 19 10,445 vs 16,577). Total events shift −0.5%, +0.6% and +1.1%.

Monthly `stats.csv` means stay within noise. One small systematic effect appears with the cadence on: Rest runs a few points higher at Iron Ford (+5 to +22), because sleepers are re-arbitrated less often in the small hours. Defenses at Iron Ford are about 5% lower (anxiety is within 1–2%): an idle hour holds a need-driven thought at its last renewed intensity, while the hourly renewal would have grown it slightly as Thirst or Hunger deepened inside its band. Story-headline counts swing by up to ±35% under the noise control alone, so they cannot resolve an effect this small.

With the prototype on, the golden 30-day chronicle lost 7 headlines (55 → 48: fewer brawls and fights, one more Sickness). The shipped branch leaves the golden unchanged.
