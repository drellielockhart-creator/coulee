# M6: first combat, lasting consequences

Planning baseline: `wave6/m6-plan`, 2026-10-12. Implements 00 P1–P8, ADRs 0001–0003 and 07 §12.5, building on `M5-PLAN.md`. **Q04 remains undecided:** tenths, auto-Tactical and firearm lethality below are recommended defaults, not recorded approvals. M6 delivers travel → encounter → fight or yield → treat → return to people who remember. Character creation/legacy remain M7; the Witch campaign remains M8.

## 1. Starting point and ownership

Current `core/src/sim/sim.ts:step()` runs needs → psyche → activity → fae → social → belief → provisions → Chronicle, then adds 60 minutes. `makeContext()` binds RNG and events to that hour. There is no tactical heap or authoritative tile movement. `activity/state.ts` has place-level transit; renderer movement is presentation. `checks.ts` already supplies d20 checks, opposed checks and Explains; `actor.ts:Sheet` lacks class attack progression and body plans.

`needs/{state,survival,survival-social}.ts` supplies Vigor, scalar deprivation Wounds, collapse, death, rescue, remains, bereavement and estates. It does **not** implement 02's located injuries, Blood or nonlethal counter. `content/base/interactions/hostile.toml:fight` is an opposed Athletics social act without injuries; `social/{interact,strife}.ts` already records humiliation, retaliation, weariness and family rupture. Preserve these consequences when replacing its resolution.

`items/{api,state,encumbrance}.ts` supplies physical stacks, supply subunits, containers and load. Weapon/armor combat blocks, chamber state and tactical reach times are missing. `psyche/api.ts` exposes `authorizeAct`, `composureOf` and anxiety. `play/session.ts` and the web worker already share PlaySession and interrupts; BACKLOG's spectator-only note is stale. Their advancement remains hourly, and place-level event visibility is insufficient for combat.

M5's region registry, journeys, itinerary accounting, T1/T2 ownership and site materialization are **dependencies, not implemented facts in this checkout**. Slice 1 can use an authored site; county acceptance requires M5's corresponding contracts green.

Register a **combat owner** for `packages/core/src/combat/**`, `packages/content/src/schema/combat.ts`, `content/base/combat/**` and combat integration fixtures. Foundation owns clocks, heap, checks, composition, unions and migrations. Needs-activities owns injury physiology and tactical positions; provisions owns item mechanics/schema; content-items authors arms. Psyche owns decisions; social/belief own consequences and law; fae owns harm permission; Chronicle, play and web retain their paths. Proposed files/APIs below require those owners' changes; cross-module writes use `api.ts`. Contracts stay additive. Unqualified core paths abbreviate `packages/core/src`; `play/` abbreviates `packages/play/src`; `content/base/` and `apps/web/` are repository-root paths. Register new schema domains through `combatDomains` and foundation's bundle composition.

## 2. One clock, resumable at every decision

**Adopt integer tenths.** Foundation adds `sim/{clock,heap,advance}.ts`: `World.time` remains integer minutes, optional `tenth` is 0–599, and an instant is `minute*600+tenth`. Never bitwise-truncate that instant. An event-driven heap orders `(instant, actorId, seq)`; `seq` is a saved, monotonically allocated integer. Store plain tagged entries, never closures. Action phases, path segments, perception deadlines, injury timers and smoke expiry schedule completions; there is no actor-by-tenth loop.

Extend `events/base.ts` with optional `tenth` (legacy zero), preserving minute timestamps and existing importance scales. Journal headers retain source/content hashes alongside engine versions.

Add a versioned `advanceUntil()` driver; keep the public hourly adapter working. **Do not call today's full `step()` once per tactical minute or after each action.** Refactor M5's itinerary into an open hour frame with interval ownership, integer carries, processed-through cursors and boundary phase. Opening an hour prepares routine intentions in existing module order; closing settles its elapsed activity, needs, production and supplies once. Split current systems into decision and settlement hooks without changing their relative module order. T0-owned actors cannot simultaneously work, shop or travel through hourly executors. Other T1 actors settle hourly; T2 retains six-hour/daily boundaries. Version this scheduling change and deliberately review its noncombat golden diff.

For live actors, accrue needs, exertion, devices and environmental exposure over actual intervals using shared kernels and carries; the hourly settlement consumes only the unprocessed remainder. Meals and production execute at saved scheduled instants, not once per tactical query. Bleeding starts 600 tenths after the wound, not at the next calendar minute. A fight crossing midnight neither misses daily work nor charges a second meal. Keep M5's objective/subjective time mapping: injury/action timers use the actor's elapsed time, county work uses objective time; save the conversion remainder.

At an instant: admit journaled Commands in sequence; resolve due entries in heap order, completing each entry's synchronous owner-API effects before the next; drain resulting perception/memory/psyche consequences deterministically; then settle any minute/hour/six-hour/day boundary in fixed order. Persist the boundary cursor. New actions have positive duration; cap free declarations per action cycle to prevent zero-time loops. An interrupt stops at this atomic instant, before any later instant. A new Command cannot retrospectively preempt a completion already processed at that instant.

**Entry:** a social fight request, explicit hostile Command or M5 encounter requests a scene at the current boundary. Finish materialization before time advances. Reserve existing actor/animal IDs and inventories; use M5's actual encounter point and entrances. Activity installs positions in stable actor-ID order on walkable cells, pins participants and suspends journeys/routine schedules. An entry record prevents duplicate admission. Hostility perceivable before an attack pauses before its windup resolves; an unseen attack can land before the first alert.

**Exit:** after six game-seconds without perceived hostility, offer the Breathe card; this is a presentation condition, not proof the scene is safe. Demote only when no unresolved attack, pursuit, grapple or acute timer requires detailed resolution. Flight crosses a real reachable exit and updates the journey through activity; it is not teleportation. Never round a three-second fight up to an hour. Preserve the open hour and injury deadlines after exit; ordinary advancement continues from that exact instant. Wait/Sleep may coarsen safe work, but cannot auto-resolve active danger or skip a bleed deadline. Ultra changes throughput, never tier policy.

**Persistence/replay:** extend `serialization.ts`, `play/session.ts` and log headers with engine/rules versions, exact command instants/sequences and elapsed tenths. Save heap, action tokens, queued orders, authorization basis/issuer, reservations, positions, partial reloads, injury deadlines, witnesses, interrupt latch and frame cursors. Save between atomic instants, including mid-windup/recovery/reload. Restore paused, without rerolling initiative or regenerating sites. Legacy format-1 snapshots migrate at their completed-hour boundary; retain versioned replay support or reject unsupported old journals explicitly. RNG gets an additive instant-aware context on closed `combat`/existing owner streams, keyed by scene, actor, action serial and roll purpose. Querying, cancelling and speed changes never consume randomness.

## 3. Rules that make gear and restraint matter

`combat/{rules,actions,spatial}.ts` provides pure resolution/preview functions shared by executors and observations. Content supplies progression profiles, natural attacks, body plans, action durations, conditions and morale tuning. Foundation adds optional sheet combat profiles with explicit defaults for existing townsfolk; no full class/feat catalog is needed for M6, and no ancestry-ID branches are permitted.

**Timing and movement:** initiative is d20 + DEX + modifiers; initial delay `max(3,30-result)` tenths, plus 30 for an unperceived threat. Unready loses DEX Defense until the first action completes. Standard attacks use 18 tenths windup + 12 recovery; draw/stand take 15–30, swift actions 5 with a 60-tenth cooldown. Recovery survives cancellation. Careful-action interruption uses Concentration DC `10+damage`; this hook supports later casting without shipping spells. Preview distinguishes cancellation before commitment from an already discharged shot.

Activity adds `tactical*.ts` APIs for Q8 tile positions, path reservations and segment ends on five-foot cells. Deterministic path ties use cost, coordinates, then ID; no corner cutting or passage through occupied cells. Movement duration uses sheet speed, load and terrain exactly once. Integer ray traversal supplies walls, cover and range; refresh perception on significant movement and sound, with staggered fallback scans. Adjacent/reach threats allow one reaction per 60 tenths when moving away, reloading, standing or using a ranged weapon; a deliberate half-speed one-cell step avoids the movement reaction.

**Resolution:** d20 + attack progression + STR melee/DEX ranged + named modifiers versus `10+DEX+armor+shield+size+dodge`. Attacks/saves retain natural 1/20 rules; skills do not. Threat-range hits require a confirmation roll. Apply range increments, footing, light, load and conditions. Half/full/improved cover adds 2/4/8 Defense; concealment separately rolls 20%/50%. Solid obstruction blocks fire. Armor's location-specific **Turn** reduces Wound damage; close firearms bypass up to four armor/natural-armor points and Turn unless proofed (heavy-ball data may specify six). Display uncertain odds as ranges, not omniscient percentages.

**Health:** add `needs/{injuries,treatment}.ts` and APIs `applyHit`, `advanceInjuries`, `treat`, `healthOf`. Keep existing `survival.wounds` as deprivation harm; add located injuries, nonlethal, Blood, combat Vigor-loss provenance and cause-specific incapacity. Effective burden combines deprivation harm with injury burdens; never reinterpret or erase an old scalar. `die()` remains the sole death path, with cause-appropriate Explain text; generalize `canAct` and recovery so relieved hunger cannot wake someone unconscious from blood loss.

Declare condition ownership: combat owns Unready/Prone/grapple restrictions, psyche owns Shaken/fear, needs owns Spent/Staggered/Unconscious/Dying and injury penalties. Staggered halves movement and permits one action per 60 tenths; Shaken applies −2. Compose modifiers once through checks, with source-specific expiry so one recovery cannot clear another cause. Register the standard combat `state/events/commands/system/api/view/index.ts` module files and condition contracts before implementation.

Lethal damage reduces Vigor to zero. Wound damage is the **largest**, not sum, of overflow, critical bonus damage, or threshold penetration: if damage ≥ effective WT, `damage-floor(WT/2)`, with WT = CON, or `max(1,floor(CON/2))` for Wounding firearms/big bites. Then apply Turn at the rolled body-plan location. Severity bands 1–4/5–9/10–14/15+ give burden 1/2/4/8. Half-CON burden staggers; CON burden or failed vital Critical save makes Dying. Recommended explicit Dying rule: unconscious, Fort DC 15 every 600 tenths; three accumulated failures kill. One-minute Medicine DC 15 stabilization stops these checks; bleeding needs treatment separately. Blood starts at CON, bleeds 1/2/4 per minute with torso escalation capped at severe; zero Blood dies. This makes a level-one musket critical potentially fatal without a PC exception.

**Brawls:** fists, gun butts and declared nonlethal strikes fill the separate counter; unconscious when that positive counter reaches current Vigor. Nonlethal criticals cap at Light, nonbleeding bruises. Zero Vigor from combat means Spent, not automatic deprivation collapse. Implement Grabbed → Held → Pinned with opposed STR/progression/size checks every 30 tenths, break/yield choices and light-weapon restrictions. Nonlethal intent stops at submission/incapacity; drawing a weapon prompts fresh threat appraisal, never automatically commands the other side to kill. Basic knife/hatchet, butt/bayonet and natural bite attacks ship; iterative full attacks and mounted combat wait.

**Firearms:** provisions adds `items/firearms.ts` and optional weapon/armor schema blocks. Start with .69 flint/percussion muskets and a single-shot percussion rifle: 20/20/30-second reloads, 1d12/1d12/1d10, confirmed 19–20 ×3, Wounding. Author dry/wet misfire ranges from 02 §9.5. Misfire uses the attack die, precedes hit resolution and has no critical confirmation; no second reliability roll. Support bayonet and club use when empty or wet. Revolvers, pepperboxes and shot cones are deferred catalog variants, explicitly unavailable for tactical firing until implemented.

Reload is a saved sequence: access supplies → charge → seat projectile → prime. Move actual compatible powder/ball/patch/cap units into weapon-owned load state through item APIs at each irreversible phase; retain their mass. Cancellation never refunds a seated charge; ignition consumes the cap/flint strike even on misfire. Recommend failed ignition retains the main charge, spends priming and requires six seconds to clear/re-prime, explicitly refining 02's ambiguous “wastes the shot.” Movement beyond one tile interrupts; hits can break careful phases. Missing caliber, wet supplies, occupied hands and container reach all matter. No modern cartridges, automatic replenishment or whole-town inventory scan.

Use real wetness/container protection; defer magical FI-only wet misfires. Shots produce wind-driven ten-second smoke, dense after three nearby shots, and spatial sound checks; hearing identifies direction/event, not shooter identity. P8's Shots preview is the minimum of compatible accessible supplies, from the same load recipe. Paper cartridges substitute their contained charge/ball once. Fouling and explosive powder accidents wait.

## 4. Agency, fear and fae permission

Combat exposes candidate intents (Bluster, Brawl, Drive Off, Capture, Rob, Kill, Feed, Withdraw); `psyche/{arbitrate,api}.ts` selects using perceived danger, drives, norms and relationships at entry, action completion and morale triggers. Reuse Self-Command/Composure and defenses; don't run the whole hourly mind system every tenth. Preserve defense cooldowns. Animals are sheets with body plans, inventory/needs and fear-led psyche data; hungry predators may Feed, wounded animals may flee. Use individual morale initially; linked pack reactions can share a perceived leader-down trigger without erasing minds.

First wound, crossing half Vigor, ally down, first unfamiliar gunfire and changed numerical disadvantage trigger Will versus `10+perceived Threat`. Composure contributes Self-Command minus anxiety band; failure bands book +50/+100/+200 anxiety. Deduplicate triggers per transition. NPC arbitration chooses cover, retreat or surrender; surrender needs an acceptance, and a cornered actor cannot flee through a wall.

Every action start and consequential commitment revalidates `authorizeAct` with its stored basis/issuer plus `canAct`; revocation cancels future acts, not already committed physical consequences. Commands may authorize up to three explicit actions; no implicit repeat attack, auto-reload, retaliatory strike or opportunity attack for a commanded actor. A bounded defensive reaction order may be explicitly granted and revoked. Passive Defense, being hit and physiological incapacity require no action permission. Fear gives the commanded PC thoughts, disclosed penalties and override anxiety, never forced flight, surrender, weapon dropping or a defense-generated attack. Q05's high-agency decision overrides older proposed forced Fits. Capture does not silently transfer agency.

Fae adds `fae/harm.ts`, exposed through `fae/api.ts`: `harmPermission(source,target,effect,instant)` returns permission and Explain using persisted forfeits, court scope and protection data. Consult it both before commitment where knowable and at impact for each affected target, including smoke/area side effects and charms. Implement only 01 §4.5's listed forfeits; attacking fae is not a new blanket forfeit. Human keeper + `harm_basis=instrument` needs no target forfeit; `fae` effects do, and a fae actor using **any** charm on a protected human still does. Hidden reasons stay hidden; a fizzle can be observed. Distinguish fae actors from concealed peoples and high Drift through data. Iron/material damage hooks call the shared 06 nature/claim weakness API, with no universal “witch damage” branch. River scope requires explicit data; never infer an exemption from a name.

## 5. Aftermath and accountable evidence

Replace social `fight` resolution with `combat.requestEncounter`; at completion, `social.api.recordCombatOutcome` applies existing respect/fear/memories and `noteStrife` **once per pair/episode**, not per punch. Persist incident evidence, witnesses and attributed identity before log eviction. Belief owns grudges and sourced claims; witnesses can disagree about aggression. Gunshot hearers cannot testify to a killing they never saw. M5 travelers/T1/T2 packets carry news to the nearby settlement at plausible arrival times. Chronicle receives a durable Fight summary, mercy, injury and death facts; micro-rolls remain in `Sim.sink`, with learned Explains retained in the session archive.

Social adds a minimal `law.ts` case lifecycle: mundane assault/theft/homicide allegation → inquiry → Arrest request → JP Hearing → release/fine/bind-over. Office holders use psyche and evidence: Arrest requires credence >70 supported by Observation/Physical evidence, not only gossip or perceived heritage. Resistance invokes Capture combat; submission is a Command for commanded actors. Custody has a place, food and hearing deadline. Hearings use testimony/Persuade/Deceive/Read People, can correct a mistaken attribution, and transfer real cents through provisions. Framing changes evidence and beliefs, never truth. No witchcraft statute or automatic guilty verdict; full posse formation and mob trials remain later.

Treatment takes time and material: one-minute Medicine bandaging at DC 10/15/20, clean dressing, water and reachable supplies; limb tourniquets carry a two-hour risk deadline. Preparation shares P8 fire/fuel/cookware rules. Add daily wound infection/healing checks, dirty-bite modifiers and persistent impairments; remedies expose known effects only. Existing care/rescue respects travel and agency; corpses and dropped packs retain their actual stacks. No conjured rescue, loot or generic healing resource.

## 7. Mergeable slices, gates and decisions

Each slice includes core, headless and minimal web play, passes `just check`, and leaves a runnable fixture. Early slices expose opted-in authored fixtures; enable ordinary county combat only after slice 4's permission and consequence gates. Estimates are focused engineer-weeks including integration.

1. **Pause at a real instant (1–2 weeks).** Clock/frame driver, heap, authoritative movement, initiative, guard and cancellation. Acceptance: scripted danger interrupts both shells at the same instant; boundary, reservation and same-tenth order properties pass; saves at :59.9/midnight/recovery equal uninterrupted runs. Fair **Greenhorn + Pocket player** explain the timeline and resume correctly.
2. **A brawl with an ending (1–2 weeks).** Melee, nonlethal, grapple, fear, surrender/flight and social handoff. Acceptance: commanded idle actors never retaliate; revoked issuers cannot execute queued effects; brawls stop at submission and stamp strife once. Table/property tests cover checks, no duplicate conditions and no free cancel/reaction loops. Fair **Greenhorn + Socialite + edge-case QA** settle tavern conflicts without designer commands.
3. **One shot, then a problem (2–3 weeks).** Located injuries, armor, firearms, smoke, supplies and treatment; PC versus two content creatures. Acceptance: every roll explainable; dry/wet misfire distributions, charge conservation, body-plan locations, Wound maximum rule and bleed-from-onset tests pass. Save during every reload phase. Fair **Catalog kid + Pocket player + edge-case QA** compare protected powder, belt access, melee fallback and medicine; record fatalities and retreats across seeds 1–10, 42 and fresh seeds.
4. **The county remembers (1–2 weeks).** M5 encounters/return, remote fights, persistent evidence, law and fae permission. Acceptance: witnessed rumor reaches another settlement through T1/T2, survives >14-day log eviction; innocent framing can be refuted; full source/target/harm-basis matrix passes. Fair **Socialite + Explorer + edge-case QA** return wounded, face inquiry and learn what others believe.
5. **Parity and scale (1–2 weeks).** Complete tactical pocket/desktop flows and versioned replay. Acceptance: identical journals/events/hashes on V8, JSC and SpiderMonkey, including mid-fight save/load, pause schedules and different speeds; payload tests find no hidden rolls, minds or enemies. Fair **all four 07 M6 personas** (robustness persona reported as edge-case QA) run authored creature/reload/witness fixtures and generated M5 sites, fixed plus fresh seeds. Browser-capable host supplies screenshots and measured 40-combatant phone gates.
6. **Balance and release (1 week).** Regression transcripts plus a year run inspect monthly needs, cash, ammunition/medical conservation, wounds/deaths, save growth and Chronicle frequency. Acceptance: 07 §12.5's five gates remain green; brawls, withdrawal and mercy produce distinguishable stories. **Explorer + Catalog kid + Pocket player** verify five-minute sessions and expedition recovery. Report combat time, decisions, lethal/nonlethal outcomes and consumables, not merely test counts.

For M6, rare consequential off-screen fights materialize their named participants/site and use the same sparse action kernel without rendering; they remain pinned until resolved. Do not keep the old injury-free Athletics shortcut as an equivalent combat tier. Background completion has bounded worker chunks but consumes real game time. T2 cannot invent anonymous deaths or resolve a second fight over already simulated actors. Profile year-run incidence; calibrated T1/T2 aggregate combat is later work if exact rare scenes exceed budgets.

Reports follow ADR 0002 at `playtests/<date>-<persona>-<seed>/`: fair/god mode, versions, command logs, metrics, quoted moments, confusion/boredom, reproducible bugs and triage. Rerun failures and fresh seeds. Determinism, agency, conservation and knowledge leaks block merging. Use collecting sinks for long-log tests; every golden update gets a design reason.
