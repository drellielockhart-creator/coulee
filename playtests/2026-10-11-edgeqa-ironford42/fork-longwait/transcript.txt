> journal
1855-04-01 00:00 Sunday, the 1st of April, 1855. The Sabbath under a full moon; the evening service will want no lantern.
1855-04-01 00:00 It has been a day. The ration is at 50 in the hundred and the children no longer ask. That is the worst of it: they no longer ask.
1855-04-01 06:00 Lydia Pratt helped Gussie Pratt at Pratt & Daughter, Outfitters. I saw it, and thought the better of Lydia Pratt for it.
1855-04-02 00:00 Monday, the 2nd of April, 1855. The full moon. The bluffs stand out white and the dogs are restless.
1855-04-02 08:00 Michael Dunleavy helped Brigid Dunleavy at Dunleavy's Tavern. I saw it, and thought the better of Michael Dunleavy for it.
1855-04-02 12:00 Amos Whitcomb drank a toast with Brigid Dunleavy at Dunleavy's Tavern on Monday at noon.
1855-04-02 12:00 Mr. A. Whitcomb, whose pantry has been bare, did not go hungry this week; Mrs. L. Trevenna saw to that, and mentioned it to nobody. We mention it for her.
1855-04-02 13:00 We are told on good authority that Mr. L. Vance has begun to dun Mr. Josiah Trevenna for $310.00. Such matters are best settled before the neighbors discover them, which in this case they have.
1855-04-02 14:00 Mr. Josiah Trevenna has settled with Mr. L. Vance, and the book is closed. A debt repaid is a quiet event and a good one.
1855-04-03 00:00 Tuesday, the 3rd of April, 1855. The full moon, which the Norwegians watch and the Cornish do not discuss.
1855-04-03 10:00 Michael Dunleavy lent money to Nora Dunleavy at Dunleavy's Tavern. Money passes, and so do obligations; I have begun to keep a list.
1855-04-03 11:00 Nora Dunleavy settled with Michael Dunleavy at Dunleavy's Tavern on Tuesday in the forenoon. One more account closed in a county full of open ones.
1855-04-04 00:00 Wednesday, the 4th of April, 1855, under the waning gibbous.
1855-04-04 12:00 Michael Dunleavy came by again with something for the pot, and would not stay to be thanked. I am learning to let a gift be a gift.
1855-04-05 00:00 Thursday, the 5th of April, 1855; the waning gibbous.
1855-04-05 08:00 Michael Dunleavy helped Lucius Vance at Dunleavy's Tavern. I saw it, and thought the better of Michael Dunleavy for it.
