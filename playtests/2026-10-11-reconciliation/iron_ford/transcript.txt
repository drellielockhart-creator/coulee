1855-04-01 00:00 Sunday, the 1st of April, 1855. The Sabbath under a full moon; the evening service will want no lantern.
1855-04-01 00:00 It has been a day. The ration is at 50 in the hundred and the children no longer ask. That is the worst of it: they no longer ask.
1855-04-01 08:00 Michael Dunleavy helped Nora Dunleavy at Dunleavy's Tavern. I saw it, and thought the better of Michael Dunleavy for it.
1855-04-01 09:00 Michael Dunleavy came by again with something for the pot, and would not stay to be thanked. I am learning to let a gift be a gift.
1855-04-01 11:00 Gussie Pratt helped Lydia Pratt at Pratt & Daughter, Outfitters. I saw it, and thought the better of Gussie Pratt for it.
1855-04-01 13:00 Lydia Pratt made a gift to Gussie Pratt at Pratt & Daughter, Outfitters. I saw it, and thought the better of Lydia Pratt for it.
1855-04-01 14:00 Hester Fenn helped Edwin Fenn at Pratt & Daughter, Outfitters. I saw it, and thought the better of Hester Fenn for it.
1855-04-01 14:00 Johann Brenner slighted Hester Fenn at Pratt & Daughter, Outfitters on Sunday in the afternoon. I was by to see it.
1855-04-01 15:00 We hear from a respectable source that Mrs. A. Pratt has begun to dun Mr. J. Brenner for $6.40. Such matters are best settled before the neighbors discover them, which in this case they have.
1855-04-01 16:00 It gives us pleasure to note that Mr. J. Brenner has paid Mrs. A. Pratt what was owing, to the last cent. The affair, which threatened to turn into something worse, has gone back to being a ledger.
1855-04-01 17:00 Miss Ellis Wayfarer had a speech ready for Mrs. A. Pratt at Pratt & Daughter, Outfitters and got through half of it. The rest was lost to laughter, at her own expense.
1855-04-01 17:00 I insulted Gussie Pratt at Pratt & Daughter, Outfitters. The room went quiet in the way rooms do.
1855-04-01 18:00 A quarrel mended today: I and Gussie Pratt. It is a small thing and a large one.
1855-04-02 00:00 Monday, the 2nd of April, 1855. Full moon, and bright enough to read a letter by.
1855-04-02 11:00 Michael Dunleavy came by again with something for the pot, and would not stay to be thanked. I am learning to let a gift be a gift.
1855-04-03 00:00 Tuesday, the 3rd of April, 1855. The full moon. The bluffs stand out white and the dogs are restless.
1855-04-03 11:00 I helped Michael Dunleavy at Dunleavy's Tavern on Tuesday in the forenoon. It cost me little and, I think, meant a good deal.
