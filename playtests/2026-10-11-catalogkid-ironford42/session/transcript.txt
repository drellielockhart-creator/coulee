1855-04-01 00:00 Sunday, the 1st of April, 1855. The Sabbath under a full moon; the evening service will want no lantern.
1855-04-01 00:00 It has been a day. The ration is at 50 in the hundred and the children no longer ask. That is the worst of it: they no longer ask.
1855-04-01 06:00 Lydia Pratt helped Gussie Pratt at Pratt & Daughter, Outfitters. I saw it, and thought the better of Lydia Pratt for it.
1855-04-01 14:00 Hester Fenn helped Edwin Fenn at Pratt & Daughter, Outfitters. I saw it, and thought the better of Hester Fenn for it.
1855-04-01 14:00 Sunday. Johann Brenner slighted Hester Fenn at Pratt & Daughter, Outfitters. I kept out of it, and am not certain I am proud of that.
1855-04-01 15:00 Mrs. A. Pratt has pressed Mr. J. Brenner for $6.40, and in company. The account is long standing and the patience short.
1855-04-01 16:00 We are glad to report that Mr. J. Brenner has paid Mrs. A. Pratt what was owing, to the last cent. The affair, which threatened to turn into something worse, has gone back to being a ledger.
